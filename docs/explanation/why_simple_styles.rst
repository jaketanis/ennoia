Why is the styling so simple?
=============================

Ennoia itself is not meant to be very engaging. It's meant to be a place to think about what you have read. Having excessive styles or features would be counter-intuitive to the goal of using Ennoia. Instead, it has a simple layout with minimal features, and text that is easy to read. This also helps with being able to look back on your book reflections in a very easy and accessible manner.
